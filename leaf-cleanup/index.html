Planning a cleanup

Some properties need one cleanup after the main leaf drop. Others need a visit while the trees are still shedding. You can ask for a one-time visit or a return visit, and we confirm availability for each.

Include your address, town or ZIP, roughly how big the property is, and which areas need work. Mention heavy leaf coverage, tight access or piles you've already gathered. That helps us understand the job before we quote it.

Before you book

Do you haul the leaves away?

Yes. We haul the leaves off-site. We confirm the areas in your quote before we start.

Can I request more than one cleanup?

Yes. You can ask about a return visit while leaves are still falling. We confirm dates and availability, and we don't assume a recurring schedule.

What does leaf cleanup cost?

It depends on the property, how many leaves there are and the work needed. Send us the property location and what you want cleaned up, and we'll give you a price.

Do you offer winter service too?

Yes. We clear residential driveways and walkways. Ask about winter route availability separately from your fall cleanup.
