How we work.

Same crew every visit

I started MD Mowing in 2023. You'll see the same crew every visit, and we don't use subcontractors.

Flat seasonal pricing

You pay the same weekly rate all season. There are no mid-season increases and no add-ons. Weekly and biweekly customers also get better rates and priority booking than one-time visits.

A price before we start

We quote recurring work within 24 hours. For retaining walls, we visit and measure first, then give you one price for the whole job.

Lawn maintenance
Weekly or biweekly mowing, April through October. Every visit includes trimming, blow-off and a touch-up along the bed edges. The rate stays the same all season, with no add-ons.
Beds, mulch, edging & retaining walls
Bed shaping
We reshape beds along walks, driveways and foundations. We re-cut the edge, clear out overgrowth, and get the ground ready for mulch or new plants.
Edging
We cut a clean edge along driveways, walkways and garden beds.
Mulching
We deliver mulch in any color you want, spread it, and rake it out to an even depth. It holds moisture and slows weeds.
Retaining walls & hardscape
We build retaining walls and garden walls in manufactured block or natural stone. We lay drainage pipe at the base of every wall, backfill behind it with gravel, and level every block or stone as we build. We also restore beds. Each job is priced after a site visit.

Answers before you ask.

Where do you work?

Lake Forest, Highland Park, Lake Bluff, Knollwood, North Chicago, Libertyville, Deerfield, Highwood, Waukegan, Green Oaks and Lincolnshire. We confirm availability for each property before we book it.

How often do you mow?

Weekly or biweekly, April through October. Tell us which you want when you request a quote and we'll confirm what fits your yard.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. Spring cleanups, mid-season refreshes and post-storm pickups don't need a recurring plan. Fall leaf cleanup is fully booked this season. Ask about next fall.

Do you take on hardscape projects?

Yes. We build retaining walls and garden walls, and we restore beds. We visit the property first, then give you one price for the whole job before any work starts.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form, email us, or call (847) 772-4803. We quote recurring work within 24 hours. Hardscape needs a site visit first.

Do recurring customers get better pricing?

Yes. Weekly and biweekly customers get better rates and priority booking than one-time visits. The rate also stays the same all season, with no mid-season increases and no add-ons.
