Services

Full-service landscape care, one crew.

Lawn maintenance
Weekly or biweekly mowing from April through October, with string-trimming, blow-off, and bed-edge touch-ups included on every visit. Flat seasonal pricing, same crew every week.
Flower bed forming
Shaping and restoring beds along walks, driveways, and foundations. We re-cut the edge, clear overgrowth, and prep the ground so the next layer of mulch or plantings sits right.
Edging
Clean, consistent lines along driveways, walkways, and garden beds. It's the small detail that separates a maintained lawn from a cared-for one.
Mulching
Fresh mulch installation — brown or black — delivered, raked, and finished to depth. Holds soil moisture, slows weeds, and gives the beds a grounded look for the whole season.
Retaining walls & hardscape
Custom stone walls, garden walls, and bed restorations. Each project is priced individually after a site visit, with drainage and course-by-course leveling built in.
Leaf removal
Full-property leaf cleanup in the fall and again in early spring. We clear parkways, beds, and lawn areas and haul everything off-site — nothing left to pile up at the curb.
Snow removal
Residential driveways and walkways through the Illinois winter. Join the list before the first storm and you're on the route from the first snowfall through March.

About MD Mowing

Founded by Marcus Dan in 2023.

MD Mowing started with a handful of neighbors' lawns while Marcus was a student at Lake Forest High School. The business is still local, still owner-operated, and still built the same way — show up when we say we will, finish what we start, and take the same care with a weekly cut that we'd take with a custom stone wall.

Today we take care of properties from Lake Forest down to Deerfield and north into Libertyville — mowing, mulch, seasonal cleanups, snow in the winter, and custom hardscape work in between. Most of our customers come through neighbors and referrals, and we work hard to keep it that way.

— Marcus Dan, Founder

Frequently asked

Answers before you ask.

Where do you work?

Lake Forest, Highland Park, Lake Bluff, Knollwood, North Chicago, Libertyville, and parts of Deerfield. If you're close to those, just call — we can usually make it work.

How often do you mow?

Weekly from April through October is standard. Biweekly is available for lighter properties. We'll recommend what fits your yard on the first visit.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. Spring and fall cleanups, mid-season refreshes, and post-storm pickups can all be booked without signing up for recurring service.

Do you take on hardscape projects?

Yes. Retaining walls, garden walls, and bed restorations are priced individually after a brief site visit so we can give you a firm number before any work starts.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form below, email us, or call directly. Recurring work is quoted within 24 hours. Hardscape projects need a short site visit first.

Do recurring customers get better pricing?

Yes. Weekly and biweekly clients get a flat seasonal rate and priority on the schedule over one-off visits.
