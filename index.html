Frequently asked

Answers before you ask.

What areas do you serve?

Lake Forest, Highland Park, Lake Bluff, Knollwood, North Chicago, Libertyville, and select parts of Deerfield.

How often do you mow?

Weekly from spring through fall is standard, with biweekly available for lighter properties. We'll recommend what fits your yard on the first visit.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. Spring cleanups, mid-season refreshes, and fall leaf removal can all be booked without a recurring plan.

Do you take on hardscape projects?

Yes. Retaining walls, garden walls, and bed restorations are priced individually after a brief site visit to understand the scope.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form below or call us directly. For recurring work we'll quote within 24 hours; hardscape projects require a short site visit first.

Do recurring customers get better pricing?

Yes — weekly and biweekly clients get flat seasonal rates and priority scheduling over one-off visits.
